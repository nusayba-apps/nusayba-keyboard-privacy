In short

  • Nusayba Keyboard does not collect, store, or send any text you type.
  • Bangla suggestions work on your phone, from a word list that is inside the app. No internet is needed.
  • Suggestions are switched off in password, number, phone, e-mail and web-address fields.
  • The app asks for one permission only: VIBRATE. It has no internet permission, no ads and no analytics.
  • The word list comes from Mozilla Common Voice Bangla sentences (CC0, public domain).

What the app is

Nusayba Keyboard is a Bangla and English keyboard for Android. It has emoji pages and a Bangla suggestion bar.

What you type

A keyboard can see the text you type. Nusayba Keyboard uses it only to type it into the app you are using and, for the suggestion bar, to find the word you are writing.

  • The text is handled only in the phone's memory, for a moment, while you type.
  • It is never saved, never written to a file, never logged, and never sent anywhere. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything.
  • To type Bangla correctly (for example when you press Backspace on a joined letter), the keyboard looks at the few characters just before the cursor. This stays in memory and is never saved or sent.

Bangla suggestions

  • Suggestions come from two small word lists that are inside the app. The app does not download anything.
  • The lists were made from the Bangla sentences of Mozilla Common Voice, released under CC0 (public domain): github.com/common-voice/common-voice
  • They hold the 20,000 most used words and some common word pairs. They contain nothing from you.
  • The app does not learn from what you type and does not build a personal dictionary.
  • Suggestions are off in password, number, phone, e-mail and web-address fields, and in any field that asks for no suggestions. There the suggestion feature does not run and does not read the text.
  • You can switch the suggestion bar off in the app's settings.

Settings stored on your phone

The app remembers a few choices on your phone: vibration on/off, key sound, key height, letter size, suggestion bar on/off, and the language it opens in. These are not personal data and are never sent anywhere. They are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app asks for one permission only: VIBRATE, so the keys can vibrate when you press them. It does not ask for the internet, contacts, location, microphone, camera, storage or anything else.

Ads, analytics and third parties

There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party services or SDKs in the app. No data is shared with anyone.

Children

The app does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

The app keeps no personal data on any server, so there is nothing to delete. To remove the app's settings from your phone, clear its storage or uninstall it.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects privacy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
